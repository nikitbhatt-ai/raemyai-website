Who this serves

Service-based businesses come in many forms but share a common operational structure: the team is busy serving the customers in front of them, and the operational layer drops the customers who are not. The category covers:

  • Trades and home services — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, mechanical, roofing, construction, landscaping, cleaning, pest control, general contracting. Typically 5-50 employees, owner-operated or with a small leadership team.
  • Hospitality operators — boutique hotels, neighborhood restaurants, restaurant groups under 10 locations, short-term rental portfolios. Typically 10-100 staff, owner-operated or with a small management team.
  • Owner-operated service businesses more broadly — pet services, fitness studios, salons, auto shops, equipment rental, event services, and any owner-led business where customer communication volume exceeds team capacity.

If your business has inbound demand it cannot consistently capture — calls go to voicemail during peak hours, quotes do not get followed up, reservations get missed, reviews do not get requested — you are in the service-based pattern and Raemy AI is built for you.

The shared operational pattern

The leakage in a Houston HVAC company, a Dallas roofing crew, a San Antonio boutique hotel, and an Austin neighborhood restaurant looks different in detail and identical in structure. Five role-based pain points across every service-based business we work with:

  • The dispatcher / host / reservations coordinator is on the phone, in the system, and in chat — all at once. Inbound volume overruns the queue during peak hours. Calls drop. Reservations get missed.
  • The owner checks the missed-call log at 7 PM and realizes how many leads went to competitors during the day. The pattern repeats every day in peak season.
  • The technician / server / cleaner shows up to a job, table, or property where the customer is not there or not ready. The team's time is consumed; the revenue is not earned.
  • The estimator / quoting team sent a dozen quotes last week. Two got accepted within 48 hours. The other ten are sitting cold with no follow-up and will route to competitors in 14 days.
  • The customer finishes a great service, no one asks for a review, and the company's local online reputation stays below where the actual quality of work would put it.

The financial impact across the category is severe. A typical $2M HVAC company is leaving $200,000-$400,000 a year on the table. A 40-room boutique hotel losing 5% of inquiries to slow response plus 8% of restaurant covers is leaking $200,000-$400,000 annually. A small roofing or plumbing company can lose comparable revenue per million in top-line in similar leakage patterns.

The Five Workflows We Automate First

Workflow 01

Inbound call capture & routing

Every inbound call is captured, screened, and routed — even when your dispatcher, host, or coordinator is on another line. Emergency calls trigger immediate handling. Non-urgent inquiries trigger an automated callback within 5 minutes. Nothing rings out. For peak-season service businesses, this single workflow can recover $40,000-$120,000+ in annual revenue from calls that previously went to voicemail or competitors.

Workflow 02

Quote / reservation follow-up sequences

Every outstanding quote, reservation, or inquiry gets multi-touch follow-up — day 2, day 7, day 14 — with personalized messaging in your business's voice. Service businesses using this sequence close roughly 40% more pending opportunities than those relying on manual follow-up. For restaurants and hotels, the same pattern applies to unconfirmed reservations and pre-arrival communication.

Workflow 03

Scheduling automation

New jobs, reservations, or appointments flow into your existing FSM, PMS, or reservation platform with the right resource, time slot, and prep checklist. Your dispatcher, host, or coordinator stops manually reconstructing the schedule four times a day and starts running it. Conflicts surface automatically. The exception management replaces the coordination chaos.

Workflow 04

Appointment / reservation reminders

SMS reminders 48 hours and 2-4 hours before every appointment or reservation. No-shows drop by 30-50% in most service businesses. Your team stops showing up to empty houses, empty tables, or empty rooms. The recovered productivity goes directly to the bottom line.

Workflow 05

Post-service review & referral requests

Automated review request fires 48 hours after every completed service — the proven highest-conversion window. Personalized to the technician, server, or service performed. Your Google Business Profile and local review presence compound over time without anyone managing it. For local-SEO-dependent businesses, review velocity is often the single biggest driver of new lead volume.
