What we automate first.

Trades businesses lose more revenue to missed calls and stalled quotes than to anything else. Every Raemy Trades engagement starts where that revenue is leaking.

Workflow 01

Inbound call routing & capture

Every inbound call is captured, screened, and routed — even when your dispatcher is on another line. Emergency calls trigger immediate dispatch. Quote requests trigger an automated callback within 5 minutes. Nothing rings out.

Workflow 02

Job scheduling automation

New jobs flow into your existing FSM with the right tech, time slot, and prep checklist. Your dispatcher stops manually reconstructing the schedule and starts running it.

Workflow 03

Quote follow-up sequences

Every outstanding quote gets multi-touch follow-up — day 2, day 7, day 14 — with personalized messaging. Trades businesses using this sequence close roughly 40% more pending quotes than those relying on manual follow-up.

Workflow 04

Appointment reminders

SMS reminders 48 hours and 2 hours before every job. No-shows on residential service calls drop by 30-50%. Your techs stop showing up to empty houses.

Workflow 05

Post-job review requests

Automated review request fires 48 hours after every completed job — the proven highest-conversion window. Your Google Business Profile and local review presence compound over time without anyone managing it.

Workflow 06

Invoice & payment follow-up

Outstanding invoices get automated reminders by text and email. Average recovery improvement: 23% more outstanding balances paid without a phone call from your office.
