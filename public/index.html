The gap

You need a senior operator.
You can't yet justify a full-time one.

Growing businesses hit the same wall: the strategy lives in the founder's head, the operations route back to one person, and the hire that would fix it — a seasoned COO — runs well over $250K a year. Raemy is how you get that leadership now.

01 · STRATEGY

The plan lives in your head.

You know where you want the business to go. But it has never been written down, pressure-tested against the numbers, or turned into a roadmap your team can actually run without you in the room.

02 · OPERATIONS

The business runs on you.

Every decision, exception, and fire routes back to the founder. Growth makes it heavier, not lighter. You are the bottleneck, and there is no senior operator to hand it to.

03 · SYSTEMS

Tools, but no system.

You have bought software — maybe even AI — but without the operating design underneath it, half of it sits unused. Technology was never the hard part. The system around it is.

Why we exist

Small businesses create the jobs.
They deserve senior leadership too.

Small businesses create the majority of jobs, most of the innovation, and most of the character of every community they operate in. They do it with less capital, fewer people, and less infrastructure than anyone competing against them.

We bring senior strategy and operations leadership — the kind that usually costs a quarter-million a year — and make it available fractionally. Hands-on, accountable to outcomes, scoped to exactly what you need. Not someday. Now.

About
Built by an operator.
Not an agency.

Raemy is a fractional strategy and operations practice led by Nikit Bhatt. Over 15 years, we have run operations across healthcare, hospitality, technology, and public safety — scaling businesses from launch to multi-million-dollar revenue, building teams across industries and continents, and standing up the systems that keep them running.

In every one of those rooms, the same pattern showed up: capable founders buried in work that had nothing to do with why they started. Large companies solved it with entire operations teams. Small businesses had one person wearing every hat.

Raemy closes that gap — senior strategy and operations leadership, available fractionally, accountable to outcomes. We work as an extension of your team, not a vendor handing over a report.
