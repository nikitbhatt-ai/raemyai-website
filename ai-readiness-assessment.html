How the score is calculated

Each question is scored 0 to 3, for a maximum of 36 points. Higher scores indicate stronger fit for an immediate AI implementation engagement. Lower scores indicate operational basics that should be addressed first, or businesses that fall outside Raemy AI's typical engagement profile.

  • 27–36 points (High readiness): Strong candidate for full implementation. Most businesses in this band see ROI within 60 days of launch. Recommended next step: a free 30-minute audit to map your highest-ROI workflows.
  • 17–26 points (Medium readiness): Good candidate for a single-workflow pilot or a strategy engagement first. The audit will identify the right entry point.
  • 8–16 points (Low readiness): Operational basics or stack stability should be addressed first. The audit can still produce a useful roadmap, but implementation may not be the right immediate next step.
  • 0–7 points (Not yet): AI implementation is unlikely to produce ROI given your current profile. Read our blog content or revisit the assessment when circumstances change.

What happens at the free 30-minute audit

The audit is a structured conversation, not a sales pitch. We will:

  • Walk through your current operational workflows in your specific industry.
  • Identify the 2-3 highest-ROI automation targets for your business.
  • Quantify the time and revenue impact in dollars per month.
  • Share whether AI implementation is genuinely the right answer for you — including when it isn't.
  • Send a written summary within 48 hours, regardless of whether you decide to engage.
