What we automate first.

Every Raemy Health engagement starts with the same five workflows — not because every practice needs them in this exact order, but because these five represent the highest ROI per hour of setup time for almost every independent practice in the United States.

Workflow 01

Appointment reminders & no-show recovery

A three-touch SMS and email reminder sequence reduces no-show rates by up to 52%. Automated rebooking outreach within 2 hours of a no-show recovers another 20-30% of missed appointments. For a $600,000 practice, that's $40,000-$50,000 in recovered annual revenue from one workflow.

Workflow 02

Prior authorization tracking

The average prior auth takes 14.9 staff hours to complete. Our workflow drops it to under 30 minutes per request — automated follow-up triggers, aging alerts, pre-drafted appeal letters, and a single dashboard that surfaces every outstanding authorization.

Workflow 03

New patient intake routing

Automated screening, 5-minute personalized response, consultation scheduling, and onboarding sequence — all without human involvement in the first 48 hours. Practices typically see 15-25% improvement in new-patient conversion rate.

Workflow 04

Insurance verification & eligibility

Automated verification runs overnight, flags issues before the patient arrives, and saves the average practice 8-12 staff hours per week. Your front desk stops chasing eligibility and starts running the schedule.

Workflow 05

Billing & payment follow-up

A multi-touch text and email payment reminder sequence recovers an average of 23% more outstanding balances than the same practice's manual follow-up — without a single phone call from your team.

Workflow 06

Staff task alerts & handoffs

Every operational task that requires a human handoff — verification flags, follow-up calls, denial appeals — surfaces in one dashboard with clear ownership and aging indicators. Nothing falls through the cracks.

HIPAA and PHI: built with a clear boundary from day one.

The honest answer most healthcare AI products avoid: many of the highest-value automation workflows can be built without touching PHI at all. Appointment slots without patient names are not PHI. Authorization reference numbers without patient identifiers are not PHI. Aggregate scheduling data is not PHI.

We build every Raemy Health client's system with a clear PHI boundary in the architecture from day one. When a practice is ready to integrate directly with their EHR — for automated charting prompts, clinical task routing, or lab result handoffs — we upgrade to fully HIPAA-compliant infrastructure with a signed Business Associate Agreement. Until then, your operational automation runs on operational data, and your clinical operations stay exactly where they are.

This is how independent practices get enterprise-grade automation without enterprise-grade compliance overhead.

Frequently asked questions

Is Raemy Health HIPAA compliant?

Yes. We build every system with a clear PHI boundary in the architecture. Many high-value workflows run on operational data without touching PHI. When practices are ready to integrate directly with their EHR, we upgrade to fully HIPAA-compliant infrastructure with a signed BAA.

No. Raemy Health's workflow layer sits on top of whatever scheduling and communication tools your practice already uses. There is no migration, no staff retraining on a new EHR, and no disruption to your clinical operations.
