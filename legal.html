What we automate first.

Every Raemy Legal engagement starts with the workflows that drive the most revenue per hour of setup time: faster lead response, cleaner intake, and never missing a deadline.

Workflow 01

Lead intake screening

Every new inquiry is automatically screened against your firm's case criteria, scored for fit, and routed to the right attorney with a personalized 5-minute response. The leads that aren't a fit get a respectful decline with a referral. Nothing sits in a queue.

Workflow 02

Engagement letter drafting

Once a qualified lead is ready to move forward, an engagement letter is pre-drafted with the matter-specific language, fee structure, and scope. Your attorney reviews and sends in under 10 minutes instead of writing from scratch.

Workflow 03

Deadline alert system

Every court filing, statute of limitations, and client deliverable is tracked in a single dashboard with multi-stage alerts — 14 days, 7 days, 48 hours. Critical deadlines never get missed because of an out-of-office or a missed email.

Workflow 04

Client follow-up sequences

Personalized check-ins, status updates, and document requests sent automatically at the right points in every matter. Clients feel attended to. Your attorneys don't get pulled into communication overhead.

Workflow 05

Invoice & billing follow-up

Outstanding invoices are followed up automatically with text and email reminders. The average firm recovers 23% more outstanding balances through automated follow-up than manual collections, without a single uncomfortable call.

Workflow 06

Pipeline visibility

Every lead, matter, and client touchpoint surfaces in one dashboard. Where your business is coming from, which matters are aging, which deadlines are upcoming — all visible in one view your managing partner can read in under 60 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Is Raemy Legal compliant with attorney-client privilege requirements?

Yes. Our architecture is built with a clear data boundary so confidential client information stays in your existing case management system. Workflow automation operates on operational data — intake routing, response sequences, deadline alerts — without storing privileged communications.

Do I need to change my case management software?

No. Raemy Legal sits on top of whatever case management, intake forms, and communication tools you already use. There is no migration required.

What types of law firms do you serve?

Independent and small law firms — family law, personal injury, immigration, estate planning, criminal defense, business law — typically 1 to 15 attorneys.

How fast can I respond to new leads?

What is the typical ROI?
