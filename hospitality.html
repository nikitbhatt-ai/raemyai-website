What we automate first.

Independent hospitality operators all face the same pain points: guest volume outpaces the team, systems do not talk to each other, and managers spend nearly a third of their time on coordination instead of hospitality. Every Raemy Hospitality engagement starts with the workflows that close those gaps.

Workflow 01

Guest inquiry response & routing

Pre-arrival questions, reservation confirmations, check-in instructions, and basic concierge requests handled automatically — with a seamless human handoff for anything requiring genuine judgment or service recovery.

Workflow 02

Reservation confirmation sequences

Multi-touch confirmation sequences for hotel bookings and restaurant reservations. A restaurant running 200 covers per week recovering even 5% of typical no-shows represents meaningful recovered revenue every week.

Workflow 03

Maintenance & housekeeping routing

Guest requests logged automatically, routed to the right team member, and tracked to resolution — without anyone manually transcribing a phone call or sticky note.

Workflow 04

Review monitoring & response drafting

Every new review across Google, TripAdvisor, OpenTable, and Yelp surfaces in a single dashboard. AI drafts a personalized response in your property's voice within minutes. Your manager approves and posts in under 60 seconds.

Workflow 05

Shift handover documentation

End-of-shift notes generated automatically from the day's operational data and delivered to the incoming manager before their shift starts. No more 15-minute manual handovers that miss critical detail.

Workflow 06

VIP guest preparation

Special-occasion guests, repeat visitors, and high-value bookings get automated pre-arrival briefings to the team with preferences, history, and personalization opportunities. Your hospitality looks effortless because the prep is done before they walk in.

Frequently asked questions

Do you integrate with my PMS or reservation system?

Yes. Raemy Hospitality works on top of Cloudbeds, Mews, Opera, OpenTable, Resy, Tock, and the major hospitality platforms. We add the coordination and automation layer; your existing systems stay.

What hospitality businesses do you serve?

Independent boutique hotels, neighborhood restaurants, restaurant groups under 10 locations, and short-term rental portfolios. Typically 10 to 100 staff, owner-operated or with a small management team.

What kind of results do operators see?

Documented outcomes from similar engagements: 2.8% reduction in labor costs, 7.7% increase in sales from AI-driven upselling, 11% increase in booking conversion rate (Distinctive Inns). 150% increase in over-the-phone bookings (Wayfare Tavern). 84% of incoming calls handled automatically (Harborview Restaurant and Bar).

How long does implementation take?

The first workflow goes live in under two weeks. Full implementation of five core workflows takes 30 to 45 days. No engineering team required, no migration from your existing systems.

Does this replace my front desk or hosts?

No. Automation handles the repetitive, interruptive, high-volume tasks — confirmations, reminders, basic inquiries, review responses, handover notes. Your team handles the moments that actually matter to guests. The goal is to give your people more time for hospitality, not to remove them.
