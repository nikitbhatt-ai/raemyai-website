Why Raemy AI exists

Enterprise companies have entire teams dedicated to AI adoption — strategy directors, transformation leaders, in-house engineers, vendor management offices. The independent business that drives most of the American economy has none of those things. The owner is the strategy team. The front desk is the change management team. The provider, the attorney, the operator, the chef — they are running the business and trying to figure out AI in the spare 90 minutes between Tuesday and Thursday.

The market that emerged to serve them is largely the wrong market. Generic AI consultancies promise enterprise outcomes at enterprise prices and timelines that do not fit. Off-the-shelf vertical SaaS products bolt "AI" onto existing tools and then leave the operator to figure out how to actually deploy them. Generalist freelancers configure ChatGPT into a workflow and call it a custom implementation.

Raemy AI was built specifically for the gap. Operator-built. Vertical-specialized. Implementation-focused. The promise is simple: most engagements pay for themselves within the first 60 days, the team you already have keeps doing what they do best, and the workflows we automate are the ones that were quietly bleeding revenue and burning out staff.

Small businesses create the jobs. They deserve the same leverage as the enterprise companies they compete against.

The Founder

Nikit Bhatt — Founder & CEO

Nikit founded Raemy AI in Houston in 2026 after 15 years operating across four very different industries: hospitality, healthcare, technology, and public safety. Most consultants spend a career going deep on one. Nikit went deep on four — and the throughline across all of them is the same: scaling operations from launch to multi-million-dollar revenue, building the systems that let small teams operate like much larger ones, and translating between the operators who run a business day-to-day and the technology teams that build the tools they need.

That breadth is the unfair advantage. The patterns that drive operational leakage are remarkably consistent across industries — a missed inbound call in HVAC, a slow lead response in legal, a no-show in healthcare, an unconfirmed reservation in hospitality are all variants of the same underlying problem. Most consultants only see one of those because they have only ever worked in one. Nikit has seen all four — repeatedly, at scale — and built the systems that fixed them.

What we believe

A few principles that shape every engagement:

  1. Operators come first. The right person in the room is the operator who runs the business — not the technology vendor. Every workflow is designed to make the operator more effective, not to remove them.
  2. Implementation is the product. The strategy slides do not matter if the workflows do not run. We optimize for things that work in the dashboard your team logs into Monday morning.
  3. Honest scope wins. We say no to engagements that do not have a clear ROI path. We say no to workflows that sound impressive in a demo but produce no operational lift. The trust we build with clients comes from being willing to lose the deal we should not win.
  4. Vertical depth beats horizontal generalism. A consultant who has implemented for ten medical practices is more valuable to a medical practice than one who has implemented for a hundred random businesses. We pick our verticals, go deep, and earn the right to recommend.
  5. Houston and Texas are home. We are headquartered in Houston and primarily serve Texas businesses. Local operators understand local dynamics — payer mix, weather seasonality, regulatory specifics, market competition — better than anyone on a coastal call. That advantage compounds in the work.

Where Raemy AI is based

Raemy AI is headquartered in Houston, Texas, and primarily serves businesses in Houston, Austin, Dallas, San Antonio, and the rest of Texas. Engagements outside Texas are accepted selectively, typically for businesses in adjacent verticals where the operational pattern matches our core model.
