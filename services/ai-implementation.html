What this is

AI implementation is the work that takes a strategy on paper and turns it into running workflows that produce measurable outcomes. For most independent businesses, this is the engagement that matters — the place where money is recovered, time is saved, and the business actually changes shape.

An implementation engagement is not a software license. It is not an off-the-shelf chatbot. It is a build-and-operate engagement — Raemy AI builds the workflows specific to your business, deploys them on infrastructure designed for the way you operate, runs them in steady state, monitors performance, and optimizes them quarterly as your business evolves and the underlying technology improves.

The 30/60/90-day path

Days 0–14

Discovery and Operations Blueprint

Workflow mapping and Operations Blueprint construction. Your team's time investment in this phase: 4 to 12 hours total, depending on business complexity.

Days 15–30

Workflow one live

Highest-ROI workflow goes live first. For most engagements, this is reminders and no-show recovery, lead intake response, or inbound call capture — depending on the vertical. Measurable ROI tracking begins on day one.

Days 30–60

Stacking workflows two through four

Three additional workflows go live in sequence over weeks 5 through 8. Most engagements break even on cost at this stage.

Days 60–90

Workflow five and optimization

Final core workflow goes live. Optimization round on workflows 1-4 based on observed performance. Steady state operation begins.

Day 90 +

Steady state, reporting, expansion

Ongoing operation. Monthly reporting. Quarterly optimization. New workflows added as the business identifies them.

What this costs

For an independent business with 2 to 50 staff, total annual cost typically lands between $25,000 and $100,000 in year one and $20,000 to $75,000 a year ongoing — roughly 1-3% of revenue. A well-built implementation pays for itself within the first 60-90 days.

Pricing is structured around outcomes, not seats or modules. The base engagement covers the full implementation, all five core workflows, the Operations Blueprint, ongoing operation, monthly reporting, and quarterly optimization. There is no per-user fee, no module unlock, no surprise renewal.

Frequently asked questions

What does AI implementation include?

Discovery and Operations Blueprint, workflow build and configuration, deployment and team training, ongoing operation and monitoring, monthly performance reporting, and quarterly optimization. Five core workflows are typically deployed in the first 60-90 days.

How long does AI implementation take?

The first workflow goes live in under two weeks. Full implementation of five core workflows takes 30 to 90 days depending on the vertical. Most engagements deliver measurable ROI within 30 days of the first workflow going live.

How much does AI implementation cost?

For an independent business with 2 to 50 staff, total annual cost typically lands between $25,000 and $100,000 in year one and $20,000 to $75,000 a year ongoing — roughly 1-3% of revenue.

Do I need to change my existing systems?

No. Raemy AI sits on top of whatever EHR, case management, FSM, PMS, or POS your business already uses. There is no migration, no staff retraining on a new core system, and no disruption to existing operations.

What happens after the implementation is live?

Workflows are operated and monitored on an ongoing basis with monthly performance reporting and quarterly optimization sessions. New workflows added as the business identifies them. Most engagements run on a flat monthly fee that covers operation, optimization, and reporting.

Can I implement just one workflow first?

Yes. Some businesses start with a single high-ROI workflow — typically reminders and no-show recovery for healthcare, lead response for legal, call capture for trades, or guest inquiry response for hospitality. Single-workflow implementations are scoped at a fraction of the full engagement and can be expanded later.
