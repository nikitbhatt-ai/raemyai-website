What this is

A fractional AI officer is a senior AI advisor working with your business on a monthly retainer — typically 10 to 25 hours per month — providing the executive-level AI thinking that an independent business cannot justify hiring full-time. The role functions like a fractional CTO, fractional CFO, or fractional COO, but specifically for AI strategy, vendor vetting, build-vs-buy decisions, and ongoing optimization.

The model exists because the alternative options do not work for most independent businesses:

  • Hiring a full-time AI executive at $250,000-$400,000 a year is not justifiable below roughly $25M in revenue.
  • Relying on the founder to be the AI strategist works for the first 12 months and breaks down as the business adds more workflows, more vendors, and more decisions.
  • Hiring vendor-by-vendor consulting is expensive in aggregate and produces fragmented decisions that do not compound.
  • Doing nothing means falling behind competitors who are systematically expanding their AI footprint quarter by quarter.

The fractional AI officer model produces senior decision quality at roughly 5-10% of the cost of a full-time executive, with the calendar consistency to actually drive decisions through the business over time.

Who this is for

The fractional AI officer model fits a specific business profile:

  • Revenue between $2M and $50M. Below $2M, most businesses do not have enough decision velocity to need monthly retainer-level advisory. Above $50M, the right answer is usually a full-time hire.
  • Multiple AI decisions per quarter. Vendor evaluations, workflow expansion, integration choices, compliance reviews, build-vs-buy questions. If the business has fewer than 3-4 AI-related decisions per quarter, project-based advisory is more appropriate.
  • No senior internal AI expertise. If you have a CTO or operations leader with AI experience, they should be making these decisions. The fractional model fills the gap when that role does not exist.
  • Multi-location, multi-vertical, or growth-stage operators. Multi-location healthcare practices, small firm groups expanding practice areas, multi-property hospitality operators, growing trades businesses adding service lines.

If the business is single-location with one obvious operational pain point, the right starting engagement is implementation, not advisory.

What this costs

Monthly retainer typically ranges from $4,000 to $12,000 depending on hours per month and scope. Standard structures:

  • Light retainer ($4,000/mo) — 10 hours per month, monthly executive briefing, on-call decision support. Best for businesses with 2-4 AI decisions per quarter.
  • Standard retainer ($6,000/mo) — 15 hours per month, monthly briefing, on-call support, vendor vetting, quarterly strategy session. Best for most fractional engagements.
  • Active retainer ($9,000/mo) — 22 hours per month, biweekly briefings, expanded vendor vetting, internal team coaching, quarterly strategy. Best for businesses in active expansion or transformation.
  • Embedded retainer ($12,000/mo) — 30+ hours per month, weekly leadership presence, deep internal team integration. Best for businesses preparing for transactions, raising capital, or running multi-vertical AI programs.

Engagements run on 6-month minimums to allow enough time for advisory work to compound. Either party can exit at the end of the 6-month term.

Frequently asked questions

What is a fractional AI officer?

A senior AI advisor working with your business on a monthly retainer — typically 10 to 25 hours per month — providing executive-level AI thinking that an independent business cannot justify hiring full-time. Functions like a fractional CTO or CFO, but specifically for AI strategy, vendor vetting, build-vs-buy decisions, and ongoing optimization.

Who needs a fractional AI officer?

Independent businesses with revenue between $2M and $50M that have multiple AI-related decisions to make per quarter — vendor selection, workflow expansion, integration choices, compliance review — and no senior internal expertise to drive them.

How much does a fractional AI officer cost?

Monthly retainer typically ranges from $4,000 to $12,000 depending on hours per month and scope. Standard retainer is $6,000 per month for 15 hours of advisory time, monthly executive briefing, and on-call decision support.

Is this different from AI implementation?

Yes. Implementation is a build-and-operate engagement — Raemy AI builds and runs your AI workflows. The fractional AI officer role is advisory — Raemy AI helps you make decisions about what to build, who to work with, and how to evolve your AI footprint over time. The two engagements are complementary and often run in parallel.

Can I have the same person for both implementation and advisory?

Yes — and many clients do. The advisory work is typically led directly by the founder when the business has the seniority requirement. Implementation work is staffed by the vertical specialist team for your industry.

What is the minimum engagement?

6 months. Advisory work compounds over time — the first 60 days are mostly orientation. The value shows up in months 3 through 6 as the cadence finds its rhythm and the decisions start producing outcomes.
