The Raemy AI Glossary.

Plain-English definitions of every term used across our AI implementation work — for healthcare, legal, trades, hospitality, and the underlying technology and compliance landscape. Written for operators, not technologists.

ABCDEFGHLNOPQSTVW

A

AI Agent
A software process that performs a defined task autonomously using AI. In a Raemy AI implementation, individual agents handle specific workflows like sending appointment reminders, drafting engagement letters, or routing maintenance requests. See AI Implementation.
AI Implementation
The work of building, deploying, and operating AI workflows in a business — as distinct from AI strategy (planning) or AI software (the tools). See AI Implementation service.
AI Readiness Audit
A structured assessment of an independent business's current operations to identify the highest-ROI automation targets. The free 30-minute version is the entry point to any Raemy AI engagement. See AI Readiness Assessment.
AI Strategy
A 4-6 week engagement that maps a business operation, quantifies the highest-ROI automation targets, and produces a written 12-month AI roadmap. Distinct from implementation, which builds the workflows. See AI Strategy service.
Attorney-Client Privilege
The legal protection that confidential communications between a client and their attorney cannot be disclosed without the client's consent. Affects how AI tools handling legal communications must be vetted for data security. See Raemy Legal.

B

Business Associate Agreement (BAA)
A required contract under HIPAA between a covered healthcare entity and any third-party vendor (including AI vendors) that handles Protected Health Information on its behalf. Specifies safeguards, breach notification, and liability.
Build-vs-Buy
The decision framework for whether to build a custom AI workflow, buy an off-the-shelf vertical SaaS product, or contract with an implementation firm. The right answer depends on volume, customization needs, and total cost of ownership.

C

Cluster Content
Smaller, focused pieces of content that link to a larger pillar page on the same topic. Part of the SEO strategy to establish topical authority.
Conflict Check
In legal practice, the process of verifying that a new matter does not create a conflict of interest with existing or former clients. Required before formal engagement under most state bar rules including Texas. See Raemy Legal.

D

Dispatch
In trades businesses, the process of assigning technicians to jobs based on availability, skill match, geography, and priority. AI workflows can surface the right assignment options without replacing the dispatcher's judgment. See Raemy Trades.
Discovery Phase
The first 14 days of a Raemy AI implementation engagement. Operational mapping, system inventory, ROI quantification, and Operations Blueprint construction.

E

E-E-A-T
Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. Google's framework for evaluating content quality, especially for content where bad advice could cause harm. Critical for AI-search optimization in 2026.
EHR (Electronic Health Record)
The digital system of record for patient clinical data in a healthcare practice. Common platforms include Epic, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, Practice Fusion. Raemy Health workflows sit on top of the existing EHR rather than replacing it. See Raemy Health.
Engagement Letter
In legal practice, the formal written agreement between an attorney and a client establishing scope of representation, fee structure, and terms. Required before substantive work begins. See Raemy Legal.

F

Field Service Management (FSM)
The category of software trades businesses use to manage dispatch, scheduling, jobs, invoicing, and customer records. Common platforms: ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, FieldEdge, FieldPulse, ServiceFusion.
Fractional AI Officer
A senior AI advisor working with a business on a monthly retainer (typically 10-25 hours per month) to provide executive-level AI thinking without the cost of a full-time hire. Functions like a fractional CTO or CFO, but specifically for AI strategy and decisions. See Fractional AI Officer service.

G

Generative AI
AI that produces new content (text, images, audio) based on prompts. Different from operational AI, which executes defined workflows. Most small business AI implementations combine both.

H

HB 300 (Texas Medical Records Privacy Act)
Texas state law that supplements federal HIPAA with stricter requirements on employee training, breach notification, and the definition of covered entity. Applies to all healthcare practices in Texas.
HIPAA
The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. Federal law governing the privacy, security, and breach notification of Protected Health Information. Applies to all healthcare practices and any vendor handling PHI on their behalf. See HIPAA Compliance for Independent Practices.
Hotel Occupancy Tax (HOT)
Texas state tax of 6% on hotel and short-term rental stays under 30 days, plus local HOT (typically 7-9%). Hotel and STR operators are responsible for collection and remittance. See Raemy Hospitality.

L

Lead Intake
The process of receiving, screening, qualifying, and routing new prospect inquiries. The single highest-ROI workflow to automate for most service businesses — including law firms, healthcare practices, and trades.
Large Language Model (LLM)
A type of AI model trained on large text datasets to understand and generate human-like text. Foundation models like GPT, Claude, and Gemini are LLMs. Most operational AI workflows use LLMs as one component, not as the entire system.

N

NAP Consistency
Name, Address, Phone consistency across web directories and citations. A foundational local SEO requirement — inconsistent NAP information across the web suppresses local search rankings.
No-Show Recovery
The automated workflow that re-engages patients or guests after a missed appointment with a personalized rebooking offer within 2 hours. Recovers 20-30% of missed appointments within 72 hours. See How to Reduce No-Shows.

O

Online Travel Agency (OTA)
Third-party booking platforms like Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb, Vrbo. Hospitality operators pay 15-25% commission per OTA booking. Direct-booking automation reduces OTA dependence over time. See Raemy Hospitality.
Operations Blueprint
The structured deliverable from the Raemy AI discovery phase. Captures every operational workflow in the business, quantifies time and revenue impact, and prioritizes automation targets. Becomes the spec for everything that follows.
Operational AI
AI applied to the recurring administrative and operational workflows of a business — reminders, intake, scheduling, follow-up, routing — as distinct from clinical, legal, or technical decision-making AI. Where most ROI lives for independent businesses.

P

Protected Health Information (PHI)
Any information that identifies a specific patient combined with health data — name plus diagnosis, date of birth plus treatment, etc. Subject to HIPAA. Note that operational data without identifiers (appointment slots, authorization reference numbers) is generally not PHI.
Pillar Page
A long-form, comprehensive page on a major topic that anchors a content cluster. Pillar pages target head terms and link to multiple cluster posts on related subtopics.
Property Management System (PMS)
The digital system of record for hotels — reservations, room status, billing, guest profiles. Common platforms: Cloudbeds, Mews, Opera, Stayntouch. Raemy Hospitality workflows sit on top of the existing PMS.
Prior Authorization
The process of getting insurance payer approval before delivering a healthcare service. The single most administratively burdensome workflow in American healthcare — averaging 14.9 staff hours per request. AI workflows can reduce this to under 30 minutes per request. See Prior Authorization Tracking.

Q

Quote Follow-Up
The automated multi-touch sequence (day 2, day 7, day 14) that re-engages prospective customers after a quote has been sent. For trades businesses, automated quote follow-up closes roughly 40% more pending quotes than manual follow-up. See Raemy Trades.

S

Schema Markup
Structured data code added to web pages so search engines and AI engines can understand the content. Includes Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, Article, and DefinedTerm schemas. Critical for AI search citation in 2026.
Steady State
The post-implementation phase of a Raemy AI engagement. Workflows running reliably with monthly performance reporting and quarterly optimization. Typical steady-state cost is roughly 70-80% of year-one cost.

T

TABC (Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission)
Texas state regulator for alcohol sales. Affects any hotel F&B operation or restaurant serving alcohol. AI workflows do not perform TABC-regulated transactions — server certification and age verification remain human-conducted.
TDLR (Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation)
Texas state regulator for plumbing, HVAC, cosmetology, and many other licensed trades. AI workflows do not perform licensed work — they handle dispatch, scheduling, and customer communication. Licensed work continues to require licensed technicians.
Texas Disciplinary Rules of Professional Conduct
The rules governing attorney conduct in Texas, administered by the State Bar of Texas. Particularly relevant rules for AI deployment: Rule 1.01 (competence), 1.05 (confidentiality), 5.03 (responsibilities regarding nonlawyer assistants), 5.04 (professional independence), 5.05 (unauthorized practice of law).
Trust Account
In legal practice, the segregated bank account holding client funds. Subject to strict accounting rules under Texas Disciplinary Rule 1.14. Trust account replenishment alerts are a common Raemy Legal workflow.

V

Vendor Vetting
The structured evaluation of an AI tool or vendor for fit, security, pricing, integration, and exit options. A core fractional AI officer activity. The right vetting upfront prevents 80% of failed implementations. See AI Consultants in Houston: How to Choose.

W

Workflow
A defined sequence of operational steps that produces a specific outcome. The unit of automation in a Raemy AI engagement. Workflows are the noun; AI agents are what executes them.
